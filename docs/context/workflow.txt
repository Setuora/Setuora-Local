# QR Inventory & Tally Integration System

## Software Requirements Specification (SRS)

**Version:** 1.0
**Prepared For:** Swarnagowri Foods & Beverages
**Prepared By:** Dijo S Benelen

---

# 1. Executive Summary

Develop a QR-code based inventory transaction system integrated with Tally Prime.

The system will allow warehouse staff, purchase staff, sales staff, and auditors to scan product QR codes using mobile phones and automatically update inventory movements.

Tally Prime remains the master system for:

* Inventory Management
* Accounting
* Purchase Entries
* Sales Entries
* Stock Reports
* GST Reports

The QR system acts as a transaction and traceability layer on top of Tally.

---

# 2. Project Objectives

The system shall:

* Generate unique QR codes for products
* Assign QR codes to existing and new inventory
* Scan products using mobile phones
* Update Tally automatically
* Maintain serial-level traceability
* Track stock movement history
* Support purchase, sale, returns, issue, and audit operations
* Detect missing inventory during audits
* Maintain transaction logs
* Minimize manual stock entry

# 26. Business Rules

### BR-01

Serial numbers must be unique.

### BR-02

A sold item cannot be sold again.

### BR-03

Purchase-returned inventory cannot be sold.

### BR-04

Serial generation must continue from the last generated number.

Example:

Existing:

SG001-000500

Next:

SG001-000501

### BR-05

Every transaction must be logged.

Store:

* User
* Timestamp
* Action
* Serial Number

### BR-06

Complete history must be maintained for every serial number.

### BR-07

Failed Tally updates must be queued and retried automatically.

### BR-08

Support assigning QR codes to products already present in Tally.

### BR-09

Bulk QR generation from Excel must be supported.

### BR-10

Phone camera scanning is mandatory.

No dedicated barcode scanner required.
